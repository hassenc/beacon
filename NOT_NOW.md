# Explicitly deferred

Beacon is focused on a trustworthy self-hosted response rehearsal. Deferred until a measured pilot need or security requirement exists: AI features, scanning, threat intelligence, CVE issuance, bounty payments, SaaS tenancy, MSSP dashboards, automatic legal classification or ENISA filing, workflow builders, plugins, SBOM management, and large-scale object-storage redesign.

Do not turn these deferrals into implied product promises. Security, export, backup, recovery and response ownership remain available to the Community workflow.
