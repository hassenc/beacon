Vulnerability disclosure policy: an adaptable starter

This is original starter language, not legal advice and not a ready-to-publish policy. Replace every bracketed field, confirm that each channel is monitored, and obtain the approvals your organization requires.

Short answer: a usable VDP policy states which assets are in scope, what testing is authorized, how to report privately, what response the organization can realistically promise, how coordination works, and how submitted data is handled. This original template is an editable starting point; the organization must approve it before publishing.

What should the policy say?

How to report a security issue

Organization: [legal/product name]
In-scope products: [products, versions, domains, or explicit scope link]
Out of scope: [systems and activities the organization does not authorize]

Our commitment

We welcome good-faith reports about security issues affecting the products listed above. We will use the reporting channel below to review a report and, where the reporter provides a reply route, make a reasonable effort to acknowledge it within [realistic target] business days. This target is an operational aim, not a guarantee. We will tell the reporter if our assessment or remediation will take longer.

Authorized research

[State the precise testing that is authorized, any rate or data-handling limits, and prohibited actions. Do not include blanket permission for third-party systems. Have counsel review any safe-harbor language and its jurisdictional effect.] Researchers must not access, alter, or retain another person's data; disrupt service; perform social engineering or physical attacks; or continue testing after encountering sensitive data. Stop and report promptly if this occurs.

What to include

Send affected product/version, a concise impact description, reproducible steps or a proof of concept, relevant timestamps, and a safe reply method. Do not include real personal data, secrets, or unnecessary exploit payloads. Encrypt sensitive attachments using [published key or agreed method], if available.

Reporting channel

Send reports to [verified monitored email / HTTPS form / other route]. Do not send reports to [unmonitored channels]. If the primary route is unavailable, use [tested monitored fallback]. Include [security contact or security.txt URL].

Coordination and disclosure

We ask researchers to give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address an issue before public disclosure. We will discuss a mutually safe timeline and provide status updates where practicable. We do not require indefinite secrecy; legal obligations, active exploitation, safety, and the interests of affected people may affect timing. Credit is optional and only with the researcher's consent.

Scope and limits

This policy applies only to [explicit owned assets]. It does not authorize testing of vendors, customers, cloud providers, or other third parties. Do not access data that is not yours, degrade service, use an issue to extort or threaten, or publicly disclose non-public details before coordination.

Privacy and retention

We will use report information to assess and resolve the issue, communicate with the reporter, and meet applicable obligations. [Describe access, retention, and privacy notice; link to it.] Please minimize personal data in submissions. Do not promise deletion periods until the operational process supports them.

Policy owner

Policy owner: [team/contact]. Last reviewed: [date]. This policy is approved for publication by: [authorized role].

What must be checked before publication?

  1. Replace all brackets; test each route with a synthetic message and confirm a named person receives it.
  2. Get legal/security approval for scope, authorization, safe-harbor wording, privacy, and disclosure terms.
  3. Confirm the listed products and domains are yours to authorize; get vendor approval where needed.
  4. Set realistic staffing, acknowledgment, escalation, backup, and retention processes.
  5. Publish a security contact file and verify the contact route is current.
  6. Run a tabletop, verify access restrictions and backups, and record the policy owner/review date.
