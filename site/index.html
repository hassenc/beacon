Who it serves

For teams that need a response process they can operate.

Beacon is a workflow tool for organizations responsible for product security and the people helping them build a coordinated disclosure practice.

01 · PRODUCT TEAMS

Manufacturer security teams

Give a small product-security function a private intake route, clear case ownership, a reporter conversation and a reviewable record.

02 · IMPLEMENTATION

Security consultancies

Explore whether a self-hosted workflow can support a customer's own response and handover. Beacon is not a managed triage service.

From first contact to evidence

A workflow your team can follow and review.

Use Beacon to coordinate the work. People remain responsible for legal assessment, product decisions, disclosure and external filing.

Private intake

A researcher can submit a report without creating an account and receive a one-time recovery token.

Triage and ownership

Staff review the case, assign an owner, record product and version context, and advance its status.

Reporter conversation

Keep reporter-visible replies distinct from internal notes; the reporter sees only the shared conversation.

Evidence and export

Review the audit history and select information for export. A person confirms the content before sharing.

What Beacon supports

Keep the operational record together.

  • Self-hosted Go and PostgreSQL application under Apache-2.0.
  • Private report intake, recovery-token access and encrypted evidence.
  • Role-based triage, assignments, status history and audit records.
  • Distinct internal notes and reporter-visible communication.
  • Human-reviewed selective exports and manually activated CRA deadline tracking.
  • English and French application pages, with no product analytics or telemetry.

Scope matters

What Beacon does not do

  • It does not decide whether the CRA applies or whether a report meets a legal trigger.
  • It does not submit notifications to ENISA or the Single Reporting Platform.
  • It is not a bug-bounty marketplace, managed response team or 24/7 service.
  • Beta and local tests do not certify production security or capacity.

Optional implementation help

Keep hosting and response ownership with your organization.

For teams that choose Beacon but need help with installation, configuration, recovery rehearsal and handover, a bounded onboarding service is being explored. The service would not include managed hosting, legal certification or 24/7 monitoring.

Frequently asked

A few useful boundaries.

Who hosts Beacon?

Your organization runs it in infrastructure it controls. The maintainers do not host your reports. Use the local beta walkthrough until your own deployment has passed its acceptance gates.

How do researchers communicate with the team?

They submit through the organization's Beacon portal and can return with their one-time recovery token. Staff messages are explicitly marked as internal or reporter-visible.

Is Beacon a bug-bounty service?

No. Beacon does not run a marketplace, find researchers, set rewards or pay bounties. An organization may operate its own disclosure policy and incentive program separately.

What does Beacon do for the CRA?

It can record human-confirmed activation and deadlines and prepare a reviewed export. It does not determine legal applicability or file anything through ENISA's Single Reporting Platform.

How can we evaluate it?

Use the published setup and synthetic walkthrough locally. You can also request a guided screen-share when a business contact route is configured.

How do I report a vulnerability in Beacon?

Use the instructions in Beacon's security policy. That route is for security issues in Beacon itself, not buying or requesting a demo.
