# Vulnerability Disclosure Program — Launch Checklist

- [ ] Named primary owner and backup; explicit escalation/absence coverage.
- [ ] In-scope products and domains are owned or explicitly authorized.
- [ ] Out-of-scope assets and prohibited activities are clear.
- [ ] Policy is approved by legal/security and matches actual staffing and capability.
- [ ] Published intake method and fallback are tested from an external account/device.
- [ ] Named human receives and can securely access reports; credentials are not published.
- [ ] Acknowledgment and update targets are realistic and monitored.
- [ ] Safe handling, access, retention, privacy, attachment, and evidence rules are documented.
- [ ] Reporter conversation, remediation decision, disclosure coordination and closure process are rehearsed.
- [ ] Backup/restore and account recovery have been exercised.
- [ ] security.txt is accurate and points to an actively monitored channel.
- [ ] Tabletop uses synthetic data; no live vulnerability is needed for the rehearsal.
- [ ] Test records and secrets are removed; public page and exports are reviewed before sharing.
- [ ] Owner and review date are recorded; next review is scheduled.
