# Community conduct

Treat contributors and reporters with respect. Critique work constructively. Harassment, threats, discriminatory behavior and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Keep unpublished vulnerability information out of public discussion. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect contributors. The proposed private conduct-reporting contact is `mailto:contact@grislabs.com`; test delivery and establish the community-response process before opening public contribution handling.
